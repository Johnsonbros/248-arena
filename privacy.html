Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 248 Arena, operated by AiSync Services ("we," "us," "our"), handles information when you use our study application.

1. Information we collect

  • Account info you provide: your arena tag and avatar, and the email address you subscribe or redeem a scholarship code with.
  • Payment info — handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store your full card number; we receive only limited billing details and subscription status from Stripe.
  • Study progress: questions answered, scores, category accuracy, your spaced-repetition review schedule, exam-simulation history, and Code Conquest ranks. Stored in your browser, and — if you are signed in — synced to our server so a new phone or a cleared cache doesn't erase months of work.
  • License Locker entries: the status of your licensing paperwork, and the work/education hours you log (date, hour count, employer or school name, and your own short notes). See section 4 for what the Locker deliberately does not hold.
  • Session ratings: when you tap 😩/😐/🔥 after a session, we record that rating with the game mode and your accuracy for that session, so we can tell which parts of the app are actually working.
  • Basic technical data such as device/browser type, used to keep the service working and secure.

2. The Examiner — AI tutoring, chat and voice

The Examiner is our AI study tutor. Two things about it deserve to be stated plainly rather than buried:

  • Text. What you type to the Examiner is sent to a language model to generate an answer, along with study material from the app. Don't put personal or confidential information into it — it's a study tool, not a private notebook.
  • Voice. If you tap the microphone button, your browser records audio and sends that recording to our speech-to-text service so it can be transcribed. Recording only happens while you have deliberately started a voice turn — the app never listens in the background, and your browser will ask your permission before the microphone is used at all. Audio is transcribed and discarded; we do not build a library of voice recordings.

The Examiner runs on hardware we operate ourselves, so ordinary tutoring conversations are not handed to an outside AI company. For a small number of harder questions we may route the request to a third-party model provider to get a better answer; in that case the text of that request is processed by that provider under their terms.

3. How we use it

To provide and improve the service, track your study progress, adapt what we show you next, process your subscription, provide support, and comply with legal obligations. We use session ratings and aggregate usage to decide what to build and fix. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles.

4. What the License Locker deliberately does NOT store

Getting licensed means handling documents that carry sensitive personal information — a CORI acknowledgement form carries your Social Security number, a photo ID carries a licence number. We do not want that information and we do not take it.

  • The Locker stores the status of each document, never the document itself. There is no upload feature.
  • It actively refuses to save notes that look like a Social Security number, driver's licence number, or card/account number.
  • We do not ask you for your date of birth, home address, or any government identification number.

This is a deliberate design decision. Under Massachusetts 201 CMR 17.02, holding a resident's name together with a Social Security number, licence number, or financial account number is holding "personal information" under state data-security law. Keeping that data out of our systems is safer for you than any promise we could make about guarding it.

5. Payment processing

Payments and subscriptions are processed by Stripe, Inc. Your card details are submitted directly to Stripe and are governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy. We receive your subscription status and billing email from Stripe so we know whether to unlock the app.

6. Scholarship seats

Sponsored students redeem a scholarship code to get free access. We ask a scholarship student for exactly one thing: an email address. No card, no billing relationship, no name required, nothing else. Sponsors who fund seats are told how many seats were used — never who used them.

7. What other people can see

If you post a score to the leaderboard, other users see your arena tag, avatar, level, title and score. Your email address is never shown on the leaderboard or exposed through our API. Everything else — your progress, your Locker, your ratings, your Examiner conversations — is yours and is not shown to other users.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers. We share it only with the service providers that make 248 Arena work (Stripe for payments, our email provider for sign-in and welcome messages, our hosting and analytics), or when required by law.

9. Data retention and your choices

We keep account and subscription data while your account is active, and afterwards only as long as needed for legal and accounting purposes. You may request a copy of your information, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us — we'll action deletion requests within 30 days. You can clear locally stored progress at any time by clearing your browser storage; note that this also clears your logged hours on that device, so export your hours CSV from the Locker first if you want to keep them.

10. Security

Traffic to and from the app is encrypted in transit. We run our own infrastructure and keep sensitive credentials off the systems that don't need them. As set out in section 4, our strongest protection is simply not collecting the most sensitive categories of information in the first place. That said, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Age requirement and students under 18

248 Arena is intended for people training for a professional trade licence. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

We know that apprentices and vocational-school students are often 16 or 17. If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian review this policy and agree to the Terms before you use 248 Arena — and if a school or program is providing your seat, an instructor should be involved. We do not send marketing to users we know to be minors. A parent, guardian, or school may ask us to delete a minor's information at any time by emailing us, and we will do so.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted here with a new date. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we'll tell subscribers by email.
